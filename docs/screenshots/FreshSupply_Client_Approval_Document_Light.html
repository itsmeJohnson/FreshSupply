Design System Tokens & Architectural Rationale

Enterprise Design Tokens
Primary Emerald #047857 • Brand & Primary CTAs
Accent Green #10B981 • Trends & Badges
Light Canvas #F7F9F8 • Low-Fatigue Canvas
Card Surface #FFFFFF • Elevated Cards
Primary Text #172B24 • High-Contrast
Border Line #E2E8E5 • 1px Structural Lines
Typography Hierarchy
  • • Primary Font: Plus Jakarta Sans / SF Pro (clean corporate geometry)
  • • Numeric Font: JetBrains Mono (monospaced clarity for weights and prices)
  • • Contrast Ratio: 11.4:1 against canvas (WCAG AAA compliant)
Architectural Principles
  • • High-Contrast Light Theme: Readable in outdoor daylight and bright warehouse bays
  • • Zero Ambiguity Dual Pricing: Eliminates manual conversion between quintals and kilograms
  • • Reefer Cold Chain SLA: Real-time sensor metrics integrated across all mobile and web screens
